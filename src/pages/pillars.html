Our Pillars

Four pillars. One shared future.

Four areas of action shape every AFCD programme, from leadership to the environment and digital inclusion.

  • 01↗ Youth Empowerment Support young people in building leadership and civic skills, participating in community life, and developing initiatives that respond to local needs. Leadership Lab 2.0Youth First AcademyLet’s Dialogue
  • 02↗ Women’s Empowerment Strengthen women’s capacities, confidence, and access to learning, leadership, and economic opportunities, enabling meaningful participation in community life and decision-making. META Skills for Women EmpowermentTOT: Gender-based violence
  • 03↗ Environment and Climate Promote environmental education, sustainable practices, and community climate action, and connect local perspectives with dialogue on Morocco’s climate priorities and commitments. Eco CitizenOxygenAl Haouz Environmental Film FestivalYouth Green Generation
  • 04↗ Digital Inclusion and Innovation Improve access to digital knowledge and skills, and enable young people and communities to explore artificial intelligence, science, and emerging technologies for learning and practical problem-solving. SAADA Initiative Project
