02What We DoActivities · Participants

How this pillar comes to life.

The kinds of activities AFCD runs under this pillar, and the people they involve.

  • 01Community outreach, awareness campaigns, and volunteer mobilisation.
  • 02Stakeholder consultations, policy dialogue, and preparation of advocacy recommendations.
  • 03Mentoring and coaching for youth-led civic, social, environmental, and entrepreneurial initiatives.
  • 04Non-formal education, skills training, and training of trainers.

Who we work with

The people behind the work.

Participants and criteria vary by programme.

  • Young people and students
  • Rural and underserved communities
  • Political party representatives and civic actors in climate programmes
  • Civil society organisations, community associations, and youth workers
03Our Approach

Local action, national dialogue.

Our perspective

Climate action starts close to home. We help communities understand environmental challenges, act on them locally, and carry their perspectives into dialogue on Morocco’s climate priorities and commitments.

Guiding principles

  • Community-led
  • Sustainable practices
  • Climate resilience
  1. 01Raise awarenessEnvironmental education and awareness campaigns with residents and young people.
  2. 02Act locallyLocally led initiatives that protect the environment and encourage sustainable practices.
  3. 03Build resilienceCommunities better prepared for the effects of climate change.
  4. 04Shape policyConsultations and dialogue connecting local voices to national climate policy.
