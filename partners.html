02Ways to PartnerThree models

Co-design change with us.

Every partnership is shaped around shared goals and the communities we serve.

  • 01FundSupport a programme under one of our four pillars, from a single cohort to multi-year work.
  • 02Co-designBuild an initiative with AFCD and the community, from the first idea to the final report.
  • 03Share expertiseTrain, mentor or host participants, and bring your organisation’s know-how to a programme.
03How It StartsThree steps

From first message to shared results.

  1. 01Tell us about your organisationShare your goals and the communities or pillar you’d like to support.
  2. 02Explore the fit togetherWe look at how your goals connect with our four pillars and current programmes.
  3. 03Co-design and launchWe agree the plan, launch the work together and report on what it achieves.
