01 / 04

Volunteer

Give time to activities in Ait Ourir and beyond, alongside AFCD’s team and participants.

What you’ll do

  • Help run workshops and community days
  • Support events and awareness campaigns
  • Bring your skills to a programme

How it works

  1. 1Tell us where you are and what you’d like to do
  2. 2We match you with an activity or event
  3. 3Join the team on the day

Who it’s for: Students, professionals and community members who want to help locally.

Become a volunteer ↗
02 / 04

Join a Programme

Apply when calls open for youth and women’s programmes in leadership, environment and digital skills.

What you’ll do

  • Leadership and entrepreneurship tracks
  • Environmental and climate projects
  • Digital skills workshops

How it works

  1. 1Watch News & Stories for open calls
  2. 2Apply following the call’s instructions
  3. 3Take part and lead your own initiative

Who it’s for: Young people and women; criteria vary by programme and call.

Ask about open calls ↗
03 / 04

Partner With Us

Co-design and fund community initiatives with an organisation rooted in Moroccan communities since 2007.

What you’ll do

  • Fund a programme or a pillar
  • Co-design an initiative
  • Share expertise and mentoring

How it works

  1. 1Tell us about your organisation and goals
  2. 2We explore fit with our four pillars together
  3. 3Co-design, launch and report on the work

Who it’s for: International, national and local organisations, institutions and companies.

Start a partnership ↗
04 / 04

Alumni Club

Stay connected with fellow alumni of AFCD programmes and keep the change going.

What you’ll do

  • Meet fellow alumni
  • Mentor new participants
  • Hear about new opportunities first

How it works

  1. 1Tell us your programme and year
  2. 2We reconnect you with the alumni network
  3. 3Mentor, meet and take part

Who it’s for: Past participants of AFCD programmes.

Join the alumni club ↗
