03How We WorkApproach · Activities

How this pillar comes to life.

The steps we follow and the activities we run under this pillar.

  1. 01Build confidenceSupportive spaces where women strengthen their capacities and self-belief.
  2. 02LearnAccess to learning, leadership training and training of trainers.
  3. 03EarnEntrepreneurial skills, mentoring and connections to economic opportunities.
  4. 04ParticipateMeaningful participation in community life and decision-making.

What it looks like

  • 01Non-formal education, skills training, and training of trainers.
  • 02Leadership programmes, workshops, bootcamps, and learning events.
  • 03Mentoring and coaching for youth-led civic, social, environmental, and entrepreneurial initiatives.
  • 04Capacity building and peer learning for civil society organisations.
