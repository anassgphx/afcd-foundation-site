About Environment and Climate ↗

Context

Residents and decision-makers in Ait Ourir describe household waste management as a persistent problem.

Approach

A participatory process bringing residents, local NGOs and elected representatives together.

Activities

Awareness campaigns (2020), then workshops on waste sorting and recycling and training for new local committees (2021).

Results

Alternative waste-management proposals developed with the community.
