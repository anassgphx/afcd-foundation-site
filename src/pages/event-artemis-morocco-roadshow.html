Artemis Morocco Roadshow

The first public event of Moonshot: Artemis Edition, an 18-month initiative empowering Moroccan youth through STEM, space innovation, entrepreneurship and U.S.–Morocco cooperation in emerging technologies.

01About This Event

What to expect.

Overview

The Artemis Morocco Roadshow introduces young people to NASA’s Artemis programme and to the opportunities of the space sector. It opens Moonshot: Artemis Edition, an 18-month initiative led by AFCD Foundation with the Moroccan Initiative for Space Industry, supported by the U.S. Embassy Morocco.

Moonshot: Artemis Edition brings together:

  • Artemis Roadshow: introducing Artemis and space opportunities.
  • Innovation Challenges: developing innovative space solutions.
  • Space Media Series: promoting public space awareness.
  • Space Orientation: building essential space-project skills.
  • Leadership Talks: learning from American space experts.
  • National Competition: showcasing and rewarding top projects.
