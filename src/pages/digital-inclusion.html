03How We WorkApproach · Activities

How this pillar comes to life.

The steps we follow and the activities we run under this pillar.

  1. 01AccessDigital literacy and skills for young people and communities.
  2. 02ExploreHands-on discovery of artificial intelligence, science and emerging technologies.
  3. 03ApplyPractical innovation activities that tackle real learning and community problems.
  4. 04ConnectExchange and cooperation that link local innovators with wider networks.

What it looks like

  • 01Digital literacy, artificial intelligence, and practical innovation activities.
  • 02Non-formal education, skills training, and training of trainers.
  • 03Leadership programmes, workshops, bootcamps, and learning events.
  • 04Intercultural learning and international cooperation activities.
