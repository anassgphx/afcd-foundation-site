How this pillar comes to life.

The kinds of activities AFCD runs under this pillar, and the people they involve.

Core activities

  1. 01Digital literacy, artificial intelligence, and practical innovation activities.
  2. 02Non-formal education, skills training, and training of trainers.
  3. 03Leadership programmes, workshops, bootcamps, and learning events.
  4. 04Intercultural learning and international cooperation activities.

Who we work with

  • Young people and students
  • Women and young women
  • Children and adolescents
  • Rural and underserved communities
