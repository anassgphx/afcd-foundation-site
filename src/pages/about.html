01Our StorySince 2007

It started with young volunteers in Ait Ourir.

From a group of committed young volunteers in 2007 to programmes across Morocco.

Photo · Youth First Academy, closing ceremony

Founded in 2007 by a group of committed young volunteers, AFCD Foundation is a Moroccan nonprofit organization dedicated to empowering individuals and strengthening communities.

Rooted in Ait Ourir and active through initiatives across Morocco, our work focuses on youth and women’s empowerment, environmental education, digital inclusion, and participatory local development.

We believe sustainable change happens when people, institutions and communities work together to create opportunities and shape their own future.

Legal name
Association le Futur pour les Chantiers et le Développement
Main office
Marrakech, Morocco · roots in Ait Ourir, Al Haouz
02Mission & VisionMission · Vision · Values

What guides our work.

A mission we act on every day, a vision we work toward, and four values that shape how we work.

Our Mission

To promote sustainable development by strengthening the capacities of young people and women through environmental education, economic empowerment, and innovation.

Our Vision

A society free from exploitation and discrimination, where everyone has equal opportunities to grow, contribute, and realise their full potential.

Our Values

  • 01 Integrity We act with honesty and uphold strong ethical principles in all our work.
  • 02 Inclusiveness We respect diversity, promote equal opportunities, and ensure that everyone has a voice.
  • 03 Transparency We communicate openly and remain accountable for our decisions, actions, and use of resources.
  • 04 Effectiveness We focus on practical actions that create meaningful and lasting benefits for communities.

04StrategySO-01 to SO-06

Six strategic objectives.

Our institutional objectives guide programme design and reporting. They connect to our four pillars; they are not additional pillars.

  1. SO-01 Strengthen youth leadership and civic participation. Youth Empowerment
  2. SO-02 Advance women’s empowerment and equal opportunities. Women’s Empowerment
  3. SO-03 Promote environmental sustainability and climate action. Environment and Climate
  4. SO-04 Expand digital inclusion and innovation. Digital Inclusion and Innovation
  5. SO-05 Support entrepreneurship and economic empowerment. Youth EmpowermentWomen’s Empowerment
  6. SO-06 Strengthen civil society capacity and inclusive policy dialogue. Cross-cutting, all pillars
