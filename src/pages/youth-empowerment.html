03Our Approach

From learning to leading.

Our perspective

We see young people as actors of change, not only participants. Our role is to build their leadership and civic skills, then step back so they can shape initiatives that answer their own communities’ needs.

Guiding principles

  • Youth-led
  • Civic participation
  • Rooted locally
  1. 01LearnLeadership and civic skills through non-formal education and practical training.
  2. 02PractiseWorkshops, bootcamps and mentoring turn new skills into confidence.
  3. 03LeadParticipants design and run their own initiatives responding to local needs.
  4. 04EngageYoung people take part in community life, dialogue and civic action.
