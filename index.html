01About AFCD FoundationSince 2007

A Moroccan nonprofit, rooted in Ait Ourir since 2007.

Association le Futur pour les Chantiers et le Développement

Founded in 2007 by a group of committed young volunteers, AFCD Foundation empowers individuals and strengthens communities through youth and women’s empowerment, environmental education, digital inclusion and participatory local development.

02Our PurposeMission · Vision · Values

What Guides Our Work.

A mission we act on every day, a vision we work toward, and four values that shape how we work.

01Our Mission

To promote sustainable development by strengthening the capacities of young people and women through environmental education, economic empowerment, and innovation.

  • Environmental education
  • Economic empowerment
  • Innovation

02Our Vision

Read our story ↗

A society free from exploitation and discrimination, where everyone has equal opportunities to grow, contribute, and realise their full potential.

  • Free from exploitation
  • Equal opportunities
  • Full potential
03Our ValuesFour principles behind every programme
  • 01 Integrity We act with honesty and uphold strong ethical principles in all our work.
  • 02 Inclusiveness We respect diversity, promote equal opportunities, and ensure that everyone has a voice.
  • 03 Transparency We communicate openly and remain accountable for our decisions, actions, and use of resources.
  • 04 Effectiveness We focus on practical actions that create meaningful and lasting benefits for communities.
