01Our StorySince 2007

It started with young volunteers in Ait Ourir.

From a group of committed young volunteers in 2007 to programmes across Morocco.

Photo · Youth First Academy, closing ceremony

Founded in 2007 by a group of committed young volunteers, AFCD Foundation is a Moroccan nonprofit organization dedicated to empowering individuals and strengthening communities.

Rooted in Ait Ourir and active through initiatives across Morocco, our work focuses on youth and women’s empowerment, environmental education, digital inclusion, and participatory local development.

We believe sustainable change happens when people, institutions and communities work together to create opportunities and shape their own future.
