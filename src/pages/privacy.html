Privacy

Privacy policy.

A plain account of what this website does with information you choose to send. It is not a substitute for legal advice.

Last updated 23 November 2026. This policy covers the public website at afcdfoundation.com, published by AFCD Foundation (Association le Futur pour les Chantiers et le Développement), a Moroccan nonprofit.

What this site collects

You can read every page without an account and without telling us who you are. We ask for information only if you choose to write to us:

  • Contact form: your name, email address, optional phone number, optional organisation, the topic you pick, and your message.
  • Newsletter form: the email address you type.

Until the forms are connected to AFCD Studio, submitting them does not send anything. The page says so. When they are connected, the message goes to the AFCD team so we can reply or, for the newsletter, send occasional programme news. We do not sell personal information, and we do not pass it to advertisers.

What we do not use

This website does not run advertising or analytics cookies, and it does not embed third-party trackers. The typeface is loaded from Google Fonts, which means your browser requests those font files from Google. Pages are served over HTTPS.

How long we keep messages

When the forms are connected, AFCD keeps a message for as long as we need it to answer you and to keep a record of that exchange. You can ask us to delete it. Newsletter addresses can be removed by asking us, and each newsletter says how to unsubscribe once sending is switched on.

Your choices

You can ask what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Write to contact@afcdteam.com or use the contact page. If a later version of this policy changes what we do, the date at the top of this page will change with it.
