What we aim to achieve.

The objectives at the core of this pillar, alongside AFCD’s wider strategy.

  • SO-02Core objectiveAdvance women’s empowerment and equal opportunities.Strengthen women’s access to learning, leadership, economic opportunities, and decision-making, and support their meaningful participation in community life.View in strategy ↗
  • SO-05Core objectiveSupport entrepreneurship and economic empowerment.Equip young people and women with entrepreneurial skills, mentoring, and connections to develop viable initiatives and pursue economic opportunities.View in strategy ↗
  • SO-06Cross-cuttingStrengthen civil society capacity and inclusive policy dialogue.Build civil society organisations’ capacities and enable young people and communities to contribute to consultations, advocacy, and public policy discussions, including climate policy.View in strategy ↗
  • SO-01Other AFCD objectiveStrengthen youth leadership and civic participation.Develop young people’s leadership skills, confidence, and ability to participate in civic life and lead initiatives that respond to community needs.View in strategy ↗
02What We DoActivities · Participants

How this pillar comes to life.

The kinds of activities AFCD runs under this pillar, and the people they involve.

Core activities

  1. 01Non-formal education, skills training, and training of trainers.
  2. 02Leadership programmes, workshops, bootcamps, and learning events.
  3. 03Mentoring and coaching for youth-led civic, social, environmental, and entrepreneurial initiatives.
  4. 04Capacity building and peer learning for civil society organisations.

Who we work with

  • Women and young women
  • Aspiring entrepreneurs and community initiative leaders
  • Rural and underserved communities

Participants and criteria vary by programme.
