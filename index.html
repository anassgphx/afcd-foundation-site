01About AFCD Foundation

Association le Futur pour les Chantiers et le Développement (AFCD Foundation)

Founded in 2007 by a group of committed young volunteers, AFCD Foundation is a Moroccan nonprofit dedicated to empowering individuals and strengthening communities. Rooted in Ait Ourir and active across Morocco.

Our Mission.

Who has benefited

Young people, women, residents and local institutions. In Ait Ourir, Eco Citizen brought residents, local NGOs and elected representatives together around household waste. Leadership Lab 2.0 trains young Moroccans in civic engagement and entrepreneurship.
