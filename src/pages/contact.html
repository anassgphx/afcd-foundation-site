Before you write.

How do I volunteer with AFCD?+

Volunteering opportunities are listed on the Get Involved page. You can also send us a message with the topic “Volunteering” and tell us where you are based.

Get Involved ↗
When do calls for applications open?+

Calls for youth and women’s programmes are announced on News & Stories and in our newsletter. Each call explains who can apply and how.

News & Stories ↗
How can my organisation partner with AFCD?+

We co-design and fund community initiatives with international, national and local partners. Choose “Partnership” in the form and tell us about your organisation.

Partner with us ↗
Where does AFCD work?+

Our main office is in Marrakech, with roots in Ait Ourir and Al Haouz. Programmes run in selected cities and rural communities across Morocco.
