01Our Perspective

Local action, national dialogue.

Climate action starts close to home. We help communities understand environmental challenges, act on them locally, and carry their perspectives into dialogue on Morocco’s climate priorities and commitments.

AFCD Foundation · Environment and Climate

Guiding principles

  • Community-led

02How We WorkApproach · Activities

How this pillar comes to life.

The steps we follow and the activities we run under this pillar.

  1. 01Raise awarenessEnvironmental education and awareness campaigns with residents and young people.
  2. 02Act locallyLocally led initiatives that protect the environment and encourage sustainable practices.
  3. 03Build resilienceCommunities better prepared for the effects of climate change.
  4. 04Shape policyConsultations and dialogue connecting local voices to national climate policy.

What it looks like

  1. 01Community outreach, awareness campaigns, and volunteer mobilisation.
  2. 02Stakeholder consultations, policy dialogue, and preparation of advocacy recommendations.
  3. 03Mentoring and coaching for youth-led civic, social, environmental, and entrepreneurial initiatives.
  4. 04Non-formal education, skills training, and training of trainers.
