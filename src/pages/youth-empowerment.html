01Our Perspective

From learning to leading.

We see young people as actors of change, not only participants. Our role is to build their leadership and civic skills, then step back so they can shape initiatives that answer their own communities’ needs.

AFCD Foundation · Youth Empowerment

Guiding principles

  • Youth-led
  • Civic participation
  • Rooted locally
02How We WorkApproach · Activities

How this pillar comes to life.

The steps we follow and the activities we run under this pillar.

  1. 01LearnLeadership and civic skills through non-formal education and practical training.
  2. 02PractiseWorkshops, bootcamps and mentoring turn new skills into confidence.
  3. 03LeadParticipants design and run their own initiatives responding to local needs.
  4. 04EngageYoung people take part in community life, dialogue and civic action.

What it looks like

  1. 01Leadership programmes, workshops, bootcamps, and learning events.
  2. 02Mentoring and coaching for youth-led civic, social, environmental, and entrepreneurial initiatives.
  3. 03Community outreach, awareness campaigns, and volunteer mobilisation.
  4. 04Stakeholder consultations, policy dialogue, and preparation of advocacy recommendations.
