About · Our Story

It started with young volunteers in Ait Ourir.

Founded in 2007 by committed young volunteers, AFCD Foundation grew from Ait Ourir into programmes across Morocco.

AFCD Foundation is a Moroccan nonprofit association founded in 2007 that promotes sustainable development through youth and women’s empowerment, environmental education, civic participation, and digital inclusion.

Rooted in Ait Ourir and the Al Haouz area, with its main office in Marrakech, AFCD works with young people, women, and local communities across Morocco.

Through practical training, mentoring, community initiatives, and dialogue, AFCD helps people build skills and contribute to positive change in their communities.

01Our JourneySince 2007

From Ait Ourir to across Morocco.

How a local volunteer initiative became a national nonprofit.

  1. 2007 Founded A group of young people committed to improving living conditions in their community founds AFCD.
  2. Roots Ait Ourir & Al Haouz Work rooted in Ait Ourir and the Al Haouz area expands through regional and national initiatives.
  3. 2020–21 Eco-Citizen & Youth First Eco-Citizen, Youth First and Youth First Academy combine environmental awareness, youth mobilisation, leadership and active citizenship.
  4. 2022 → Leadership Lab Successive editions of Leadership Lab develop this work through training, mentoring, entrepreneurship and support for community initiatives.
02What We DoAreas · Activities

Programmes combine non-formal education, practical training, mentoring and community action.

Core activities

  1. 01Non-formal education, skills training, and training of trainers.
  2. 02Leadership programmes, workshops, bootcamps, and learning events.
  3. 03Mentoring and coaching for youth-led civic, social, environmental, and entrepreneurial initiatives.
  4. 04Community outreach, awareness campaigns, and volunteer mobilisation.
  5. 05Stakeholder consultations, policy dialogue, and preparation of advocacy recommendations.
  6. 06Digital literacy, artificial intelligence, and practical innovation activities.
  7. 07Capacity building and peer learning for civil society organisations.
  8. 08Intercultural learning and international cooperation activities.
